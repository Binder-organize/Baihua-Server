# Security Policy

---

## Reporting a Security Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in Baihua, please send a detailed report by email to:

**Gavin Zheng <gav.zheng@outlook.com>**

This dedicated address ensures your report will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially by our security team.

## When in Doubt, Report

If you are unsure whether an issue constitutes a security vulnerability, we strongly encourage you to report it anyway. Please do not discuss potential vulnerabilities publicly until we have had a chance to review and address them.

## Confidentiality

All discussion about vulnerabilities should take place exclusively through the email address above. We will not publicly disclose vulnerabilities until an official announcement is made through our authorized communication channels.

## What to Include in Your Report

To help us respond quickly and effectively, please include the following information in your report:

1. **Proof of concept** — A concise description or script demonstrating how the vulnerability can be exploited, if possible. This helps us understand the nature and severity of the issue.

2. **Affected platforms and scenarios** — Specify the platforms (operating systems, hardware configurations, software versions) where the vulnerability is likely to be exploitable.

3. **Researcher information** — Your name and affiliation (if applicable). This information is kept confidential and used only for internal tracking.

4. **Disclosure status** — Let us know if the vulnerability has already been disclosed to any third party. This helps us prioritize and scope our response.

5. **Suggested embargo period** — Your recommendation on how long an embargo should be in place before public disclosure. This guides our decision-making on the timing of an official announcement.
